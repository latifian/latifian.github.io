news: "Our paper “<a href='/publications/#PacingStrategies'>Nash Equilibria in Auctions with Pacing Strategies: Complexity and Inefficiency</a>,” with Aris Filos-Ratsikas and Charalampos Kokkalis, has been accepted at WINE ’26."
details: "In this work, we study first-price auctions in which each bidder chooses a single multiplier that scales all their bids. We show that even approximate pure Nash equilibria may fail to exist, and deciding whether they exist is NP-complete. However, this problem becomes tractable when either the number of bidders or the number of items is fixed. For instances admitting a pure Nash equilibrium, we establish tight bounds on social welfare: both the price of anarchy and the price of stability equal e/(e−1)."
