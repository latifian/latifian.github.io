news: "Happy to share that our paper <a href='https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00149'>Optimal Metric Distortion for Matching on the Line</a> was accepted for publication at IJCAI '25."
details: "In this work, we study one-sided and two-sided matching problems on the line using only ordinal preference information. For one-sided matching, we give an algorithm that achieves the optimal distortion of 3 simultaneously for several social-cost objectives, including the utilitarian and egalitarian objectives. For two-sided matching, we show that an optimal matching can be computed with even less information and establish bounds on the number of required queries."
