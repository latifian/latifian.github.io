news: "Happy to share that our paper <a href='https://doi.org/10.1145/3774904.3792650'> Social Deliberation: Democratic Deliberation Through Unbiased Social Interactions </a> was accepted for publication at WWW '26."
details: "In this work, we argue that when voters are public-spirited and communicate without bias, their everyday interactions can be viewed as a form of deliberation. We model these interactions as a networked information-aggregation process that culminates in a vote. We show that, under mild assumptions on the network and the communication process, the resulting voting outcome achieves constant distortion—that is, its social welfare remains within a constant factor of the optimum."
